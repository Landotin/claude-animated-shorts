You are nine years old, and your own tribe just left you in the freezing Mongolian dirt to starve to death.

Big mistake. <short pause>
You rally forty thousand horsemen and wipe the Merkits off the map.

But uniting the warring steppes isn't enough.
You rewrite the rules of war. You abolish aristocracies -- if a beggar fights like a god, you make him a general.
Next target: Northern China.
They hide behind forty-foot stone walls and laugh at your nomads.
Therefore... you starve them out, steal their engineers, and turn their own catapults against them until Beijing burns for a month straight. <short pause>

You want trade with the Persian Empire, so you send a peaceful caravan.
Their governor executes your messengers and sends their heads back in a sack. <short pause>
So you unleash hell.
Two hundred thousand riders cross the desert. You divert entire rivers to flood their cities off the earth.
You conquer more territory in twenty-five years than the Romans did in four hundred. <long pause>

Then, at sixty-five, a sudden fever and an old war wound finally catch up to you. <sigh>
As you take your final breath, you give your sons one terrifying last order: <short pause>
Keep your death a secret, bury you in an unmarked grave, and slaughter anyone who sees your funeral procession. <short pause>
You arrived in this world with nothing. You leave behind the largest contiguous empire on earth... <short pause> and nobody even knows where your bones are buried.
